About i@masjid

Our mission, vision & the team behind the platform

Our Mission

To digitally empower mosques across Malaysia by providing an accessible, intuitive platform that strengthens community bonds and facilitates Islamic learning and worship.

Our Vision

To be the leading digital ecosystem for mosques in Malaysia — connecting every Muslim with their local masjid through modern technology while preserving Islamic values.

Our Values

Built on the pillars of transparency, inclusivity, and service to the ummah. Every feature is designed with the needs of the Muslim community at heart.

The i@masjid Story

i@masjid was born as a Final Year Project at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM) with the goal of bridging the gap between mosques and their communities using modern web technology. Today, it serves as a comprehensive platform offering prayer schedules, event management, member registration, and more — all in one place.

The name i@masjid represents information at the mosque — fast, accessible, and always available, just like the masjid itself.
